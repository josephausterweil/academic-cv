## Notes for Data Entry

1. **Career History**: Enter positions in reverse chronological order (most recent first)
2. **Education**: Make sure to include the year of degree completion
3. **Awards**: Include the full official name of each award
4. **Grants**: Include funding agency, project title, your role, and dates
5. **Teaching**: Focus on recent courses (last 5-10 years)
